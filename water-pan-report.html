Water pan suitability V03: inputs and method

KAZA TFCA, Zambezi Region, Namibia. This page lists every input layer, how it is grouped and weighted, and the processing settings behind the V03 suitability scores shown in the explorer.

How the score is built

Fourteen layers are brought onto one 100 m grid, each rescaled to a 0–1 score where higher means more suitable, and combined in two steps: layers into three group scores, then groups into one suitability score per scenario. Results are produced as 100 m rasters and as averages per hexagon (about 1 km² each, 12,922 hexagons).

1. Preparev03_prepare_data.RAlign every layer to the shared grid, clip to the study area, build the land productivity and ephemeral-water layers.
2. Rescalev03_score.RClip each layer to its 2nd–98th percentile and stretch to 0–1; flip layers where lower is better.
3. Combinev03_score.RWeighted average within each group, then weighted average of the three groups per scenario.
4. Maskv03_build_landcover_masks.ROptionally keep only hexagons dominated by grass, bare ground or shrub.

Scenarios

A scenario is a set of group weights. The balanced scenario treats the three groups equally; each emphasis scenario gives one group 0.6 and the other two 0.2.

What changed from V02

  • Six groups became three. Climate and Cost were dropped; Topography & Soil and Land Surface Condition were merged into Soil and degradation.
  • Removed layers: clay, RPI, slope, land surface temperature, bare soil index, grass and bare fraction, the five climate layers, the three access-road layers, woody cover and micro-corridors.
  • New layer: land productivity dynamics (degraded share).
  • Ephemeral water is rebuilt from the 10 m mosaic with wetlands included; V02 used a version with large water bodies cut out.
  • No exclusion zones are applied. The land-cover mask is an optional filter on the results, not part of the score.

Reading the results

  • Scores are relative to this study area. A score of 0.8 means "among the best cells here for that layer", not an absolute standard.
  • Rescaling is done per layer across all cells, so adding or removing area would shift the scores.
  • The mask and the score are independent: masking hides hexagons but does not change the score of those that remain.
