How this was made

Water. Ephemeral surface water recurrence (Sentinel-2, 2020–2025) per season: early wet (Feb–May), mid dry (May–Jul), late dry (Jul–Sep). Values are counted as years wet per season (0–6). Patches touching an OSM river (100 m bridge, 5 km search buffer) were treated as river-connected flood water and removed.

Shortlist. Isolated patches ≤ 10 ha, wet in ≥ 2 season-years and in mid-dry in ≥ 2 years: 39 of 1,848 patches. “Max” values describe the most persistent pixel (the core of the hollow).

Site type. Near-permanent: wet in late dry in ≥ 4 years (already works as a pan). Deepening target: holds water into mid-dry but rarely into late dry, so deepening could extend it.

People. TUM Global Building Atlas footprints. Settlements are clusters of ≥ 20 buildings less than 100 m apart. Distances are from the patch edge.

Context. Roads from OpenStreetMap; emerging communal conservancies (Zilitene, Kabbe, Lake Lyambezi); the dotted red line is the boundary of the wider Zambezi study area. Road distance is from the patch edge to the nearest mapped road of any class.

Field checks. Soil (clay content), current depth, livestock and wildlife use, land tenure, and whether water near homesteads raises human–elephant conflict risk.
